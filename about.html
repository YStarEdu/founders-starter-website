About Crumb

A two-person bakery on a quiet corner, open since 2019.

Crumb started with one oven and a sourdough starter called Bubbles. Today we bake bread, pastries and cookies every morning, in batches small enough that everything is sold by the end of the day.

Ordering ahead means we bake what people actually want, and you never find the shelf empty. Leftovers go to the community fridge down the street.
